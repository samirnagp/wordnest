Questions

Quick answers before you download.

Are the templates really free?

Yes. You can use them for personal and business documents without attribution.

Which programs can open them?

Microsoft Word 2010 or newer, Word for the web, Google Docs, LibreOffice and Apple Pages.

How do I fill in a template?

Click each [bracketed] text and type over it. Delete any line you don’t need.

Can I save it as a PDF?

In Word choose File, then Save As, then PDF. Always check the document once before you send it.
